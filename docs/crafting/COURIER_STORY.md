# The ancient ledger — courier story proposal

Designer spoilers. Names, counts and rewards below are proposals; the book-on-acceptance, varied travel quests, Mistvale apparent ending and first-use desert revelation are confirmed direction.

The 18 regional folios contain all 200 component entries and 180 named assemblies. They reveal knowledge, not crafting permission. After the first Blackthorn commission the player chooses accessible routes; the Mire record must precede its Hollow follow-up. Mistvale requires six prior folios to offer its commission and eight total to restore the forge. Other folios can be found afterward.

## The Last Ember of the Old Craft

**Gate:** courier_mistvale_completed; at_least_8_regional_pages_from_8_territories; courier_stormcrown_keep_completed OR courier_copperfen_basin_completed

Recover the aqueduct’s focusing ring; deliver 6 Veil Silver, 4 Thundersteel and 4 Springbrass; restore the channels and defeat the relic guardian.

Mistvale relic forge unlocked; apparent finale, celebration and complete journal entry. No secret-forge silhouette or percentage-complete hint.

Unlock the hidden chapter and offer its three investigations simultaneously.

**Gate:** sands_lens_completed; sands_pressure_completed; sands_memory_completed

## Integration rules

- **Optional:** Never gates main campaign liberation, travel or hero recruitment.
- **Order:** After the opening commission, regional commissions can be accepted and completed in any accessible order except the linked Mire/Hollow record task and Mistvale milestones.
- **Pages:** One regional reward is a folio containing several leaves, not a single tiny page. All 18 folios have explicit rewards; 8 are sufficient to approach Mistvale. The player can keep collecting the others afterward.
- **Book:** Granted on first mission acceptance, before its completion. Recipe names and hidden numbers appear only when earned. Discovery never creates an item or unlocks an unknown source automatically.
- **Delivery:** Gathered objectives accept previously collected ordinary stock. Named quest objects are bound, non-sellable and cannot be irreversibly lost. Rare core objectives explicitly warn of consumption.
- **Clocks:** No expiration, real-time countdown, daily rotation, or forced grinding to retain progress.
- **Curse:** Both Mire and Hollow remain unliberated until their shared main-story climax; courier record recovery does not cleanse either map.
- **Spoilers:** Sands investigations are absent from player quest menus, completion counts, map pins and NPC hints until book_awakening.
- **Access:** No mission requires recruiting a specific hero. Resident hero and guild contacts remain available on either branch.
- **Recovery:** If a required battle is lost, objectives remain retryable; consumed turn-in goods commit with rewards atomically.
