# Weapon-family assembly — 2026-10-12

The active Foundry mounts the selected framework and striker for all 15 catalog families, including named recipes. Named recipe statistics are preserved, while their unrelated finished-weapon concept icons no longer replace the selected parts. Core effects remain pending.

Sword keeps its existing per-item calibration in `sword_attachment_points.json`. Other families resolve the selected PNG through `weapon_attachment_points.json`: 132 distinct source-image records cover originals and shared placeholders. Each record specifies source region, socket, angle, scale, and where necessary mirrored placement or source-fragment exclusions. Families define insertion overlap and which part draws in front. Bows compose the two source limb regions above and below the riser; crossbows, gauntlets and cannons have separate mounting orientations. No visible dots are drawn.

The catalog still has 176 expansion art placeholders. A shared placeholder intentionally shares its source-image attachment record. Whenever replacement art is installed, add its own record before changing the catalog path. Missing records never substitute a different component. This is the first family-wide visual calibration pass; complex older source images still need visual refinement and better component art. Passing geometry checks does not establish that every join is visually final.

Six new Sword blades are installed as 512 x 512 RGBA PNGs under `assets/entropic_forge/icons/strikers/`: Ravenwood Thorn Sabre, Ashen Magma Edge, Icehelm Crystal Longblade, Ironhold Breaker Falchion, Stormcrown Lightning Rapier and Veiled Sea Boarding Cutlass. Generated with built-in image_gen and normalized with preserved alpha. Their inventory thumbnails and assembled geometry use the same files. The rejected Ironhold halo variants were not installed. Queue, source hashes and art metadata are recorded in `component_art_jobs.json`.

Validation: headless Godot import; Sword image-identity and 120-pair regression; live result construction and socket/layout checks for 1,800 pairs across 15 families at three viewport sizes; inventory smoke; catalog/art integrity. Independent reference composition sheets were visually inspected for every family and the six replacement blades; these are not native gameplay screenshots. The Android pipeline runs the new `tests/weapon_socket_smoke.gd` gate.
